About Baladi Laban

Welcome to Baladi Laban — where authentic Middle Eastern flavors meet a modern dessert experience. Inspired by the rich streets of Egypt and the warmth of Arabic hospitality, we bring you indulgent drinks, desserts, and signature creations crafted with premium ingredients and unforgettable taste. At Baladi Laban, every dish is made to deliver freshness, quality, and a unique experience that blends tradition with innovation. From creamy milk-based specialties to refreshing beverages and loaded desserts, our menu is designed for people who love bold flavors and memorable moments. Founded by Shabnam shuaib Bari, the journey began with immersive research trips across Egypt, diving deep into local dessert markets, sourcing genuine ingredients, and collaborating with expert chefs. Our mission is simple — to create a brand that feels luxurious, welcoming, and full of culture while serving products that keep our customers coming back for more. Whether you visit us for a quick craving, a family outing, or to explore something new, Baladi Laban promises an experience filled with taste, creativity, and authenticity. Taste the Tradition.
